What this module does for your project

Your Project Brief is the theory section of a research paper. Module 1 writes the Data & Methods section: what one row of your data is, which variables measure your concepts and what kind of thing each one is, where the data comes from (or how you will honestly simulate it), what could go wrong with it, and a first description of your key variables.

No formulas, and no code. This module is about translation: taking a concept and deciding what it looks like as something you can measure.
