What this module does for your project

Module 1 wrote your Data & Methods section. Module 2 writes Results: at least one chart, an association finding, and an honest statement of what it does and does not establish.

Describing one variable at a time can't show you a relationship. This module moves from one variable to two: what question a chart answers, what a group average hides, and what it means when two things move together. It stays on the first rung of Pearl's ladder, seeing, and ends by naming exactly why that rung isn't enough. Module 3 climbs.

Still no formulas, and no code. Your AI analyst draws the charts and computes the numbers. You choose the question, and you say what the answer means.
