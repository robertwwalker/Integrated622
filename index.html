The course in one idea

Data is the measurement of models. We do not collect data and then wonder what it means. We build models of how organizations work, and data is how we find out whether those models are telling us the truth. There’s also forecasting: models provide us with exactly what we need to forecast our uncertain world in a careful and coherent way.

You arrive from organizational management or operations management with a partially formed project. Over three modules you will define what to measure, find or honestly simulate the data, analyze it, and reason carefully about what the evidence can and cannot establish. AI does the technical work, in plain language. The thinking is yours.

An MBA is strategy, and every discipline in it (finance, accounting, operations, organizations, marketing, HR, information systems, and data) is a model that strategy links together. This course is where those models meet data: to test what we believe, and to forecast what will happen if we act. Read: Models and the MBA.
