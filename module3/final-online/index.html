Pod review

Each reviewer comments directly in the document, on the work, not the author. Pods support; they don't co-author. Answer these, each in a comment where it applies:

  1. What is the claim, in one sentence? Does the first paragraph state it?
  2. Which rung does the evidence reach? Is it named?
  3. What would it take to know more, and is it worth it?
  4. What's the forecast, as a range, and what does it assume?
  5. Do the slides (or the draft video) make the same claim, with the same limits, as the paper? For a video: is it within the agreed length, and does it say anything that isn't in the paper?
  6. One sentence a decision-maker would misread, and your suggested rewrite.

End with one overall comment, from the decision-maker's chair: "On Monday, I would… because…"

Round 2: a 10-minute draft video at 1.25× speed takes 8 minutes to watch. If there's no draft video, review the slides: where does the biggest limitation appear?

Your fix list (10 minutes)

Read the comments on your own work. Write the three changes you'll make before Friday, starting with any place a reviewer misread your claim. You don't have to take every suggestion. You do have to know why you didn't.

Concluding remarks

We started with one idea: data is the measurement of models. Over three modules, you wrote a model of part of an organization, decided what to measure, looked at what the data shows, and asked what it can and can't establish.

The takeaway, previewed in Projects at the boundary: data can tell you what goes with what. Only data plus a model you are willing to defend can tell you what will happen if you act. AI makes the first part cheap. That makes the second part more important, not less.

And one causal question we've carried all term. This course is a redesign, with two changes at once, one cohort, and no comparable baseline. You've just filled in its evaluations: first-rung data about a change, with no comparison group. What would it take to move this course's evaluation up a rung? You know the questions to ask now.

All models are wrong. The useful ones say how. Thank you for a term of puzzles.
