Readings

About two hours of reading before each of the first two sessions, and a lighter set for the dry run, each with an active reading guide. One book runs through the module, Huntington-Klein's The Effect, free online: what a causal diagram is, how to draw your own, and how to compare changes instead of levels. Pearl's ladder opens the module, and Hubbard's chapter on the value of information finishes the thread from Module 1.

Module 3 readings, reading guides, and the full sequence of what's due →

What this module does for your project

Module 1 wrote your Data & Methods section, and Module 2 wrote Results. Module 3 writes Interpretation, then puts the whole project together: your causal claim, the rung of Pearl's ladder your evidence reaches, what it would take to know more, and a forecast for the decision you recommend.

Module 2 stayed on the first rung, seeing: what goes with what. Module 3 climbs as far as your evidence allows, and says honestly where it stops. Still no formulas, and no code.
